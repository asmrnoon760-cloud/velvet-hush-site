Privacy Policy

Velvet Hush Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to their own YouTube channel.

Data we access

The app requests permission only to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel (youtube.upload scope). It does not read, collect or store any other Google account data.

Data sharing

No data is sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties. Access tokens are stored privately by the owner and used only to perform uploads.

Contact

For questions, contact the channel owner through the Velvet Hush ASMR UB YouTube channel.
